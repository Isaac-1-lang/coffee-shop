Background

At Jane's Coffee Shop, everyone is welcome!

Located in Kicukiro-Kanombe, Jane's Coffee Shop is a hub of talent. We serve high-quality coffee and also support singing, dancing, and drawing competitions. Our vision is to produce the best coffee, reduce unemployment, and improve living standards locally and worldwide.

Services

  • Daily delivery of coffee and related products
  • Daily mentoring of employees
  • Talent competitions for the youth
  • Community events and gatherings

Innovation

Our main aim is not just profit. We focus on improving the world, growing our business responsibly, and providing quality products while enhancing society’s welfare. We innovate by combining premium coffee experiences with community support and talent nurturing.
